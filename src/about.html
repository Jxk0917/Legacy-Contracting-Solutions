What we do

Legacy Contracting Solutions is a contracting and site services company working in San Antonio and the surrounding areas. The work covers excavation and site preparation, concrete, fencing, hauling and material delivery, and demolition and removal.

Customers are homeowners, contractors, pool builders, landscapers and commercial clients. A pool builder who needs a hole dug and a homeowner who needs a patio poured are both normal weeks.

How the work runs

Jobs are quoted before they start. Scope and price are agreed up front, the crew does the work, and what is left over gets hauled off rather than left in the yard.

Because the equipment and the crew are the same from digging through cleanup, a job that would normally be split across several trades can stay with one number.

Built from the ground up.

Where we work

San Antonio is the primary area. Recent projects are documented in Boerne, Bandera and Fair Oaks. For work outside those areas, call and ask.
